Plane Radar

Live ADS-B radar for a CYD, a NiceMCU 2.8" or a round ESP32-S3 display. Plug the board in by USB, click the Install button under YOUR board, pick the serial port. The CYD and the NiceMCU are both plain ESP32 boards, so use the button that matches the board you have. Chrome or Edge on a computer.

CYD 2.4" (ESP32-2432S024R)

Radar, info strip, touch, RGB LED, light sensor.

This browser can't flash. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Hold the BOOT button on the back for the whole install, then press RST. Without it the install stops with a boot-mode error.

NiceMCU 2.8" (NiceMCU-32S-DEV)

240x320 IPS, capacitive touch (no calibration), RGB LED.

This browser can't flash. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

No buttons needed. If the port doesn't show up, unplug and plug it back in.

Round 1.85" (Guition JC3636W518C)

360 px round radar, capacitive touch.

This browser can't flash. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

No buttons needed. If the port doesn't show up, unplug and plug it back in.

After installing

  1. CYD only: calibrate the touch screen. Drag from each corner arrow toward the centre.
  2. Join the Wi-Fi network shown on the screen (PlaneRadar-CYD, PlaneRadar-NiceMCU or PlaneRadar-Round) from a phone and open 192.168.4.1.
  3. Pick your Wi-Fi, enter the password and your latitude and longitude (6 decimals at most), and save.

Source and details: github.com/TLaren/ESP32-Plane-Radar
